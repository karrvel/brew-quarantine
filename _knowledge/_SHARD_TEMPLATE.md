# A short heading restating the trap, decision, finding or task

Two to five tight sentences. For a **gotcha**: what breaks, the symptom, and the fix or the why. For a **decision**: context, decision, rationale (why X and not Y), consequences. For **security**: the rule or the open finding, and how to verify it. For a **task**: the concrete remaining work and its acceptance criteria.

Be actionable and cite specifics — a file, a function, a measured value. Link related shards with `[[other-slug]]`. Prefer zero shards over noise: generic best practice that everyone already knows does not belong here.
